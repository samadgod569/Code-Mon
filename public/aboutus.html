About Code-Mon

Who We Are

Code-Mon is a modern, secure, and developer-focused website hosting platform designed to make building, managing, and deploying websites simple, fast, and safe. We provide tools for file management, deployment, APIs, and scalable infrastructure — all under a security-first architecture.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a powerful hosting environment that prioritizes data safety, performance, and developer freedom. Code-Mon is built to give creators full control over their content while maintaining strict internal security standards.

Security & Data Protection

At Code-Mon, protecting user data is a core principle. All hosted content and user data are handled with care and are protected by multiple layers of access control.

To ensure maximum security and prevent unauthorized access, only one trusted internal member is allowed to redirect or manage sensitive system-level data. This design minimizes risk and ensures accountability at every level of the platform.

Transparency & Trust

We believe trust is built through transparency. Code-Mon does not sell user data, does not perform hidden tracking, and does not allow unauthorized third-party access to hosted content.
